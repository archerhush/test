The selection jumps into the wrong box

1. Select a few words in the left or right box, then enter Tridactyl visual mode.

2. Press 3j again and again, even after the last line.

3. If the selection jumps into another box, press k to try to go back.

The problem: the selection leaves the box, and pressing k does not bring it back. You have to press Esc and start again.

The middle box is for comparison: in your last test, it stopped at the end instead.
